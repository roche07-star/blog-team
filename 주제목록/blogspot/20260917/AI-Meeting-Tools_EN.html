Fathom vs Fireflies.ai vs Granola: The Right AI Meeting Tool for Your Team

Meeting recording and transcription tools have become table stakes in modern work. But which one actually delivers the features your team needs without burning through your budget?

This September 2026 comparison cuts through the noise and looks at three of the strongest contenders: Granola (the bot-free specialist), Fathom (the freemium generalist), and Fireflies.ai (the integration powerhouse). We'll compare pricing, capture quality, feature sets, and real-world use cases so you can pick the right fit.

01

The State of AI Meeting Tools in 2026

The meeting capture landscape has matured significantly. As of September 2026, the key differentiator isn't just whether a tool can transcribe—it's how it captures audio and what automation it builds on top.

Three distinct capture models now dominate:

  • Bot-based capture: A virtual participant joins your call and records directly from the meeting. Most accessible, works on any platform, but meeting members see the bot is present.
  • Bot-free (device-level) capture: Records directly from your system audio and microphone. Invisible to other participants. Privacy-focused but more technically complex.
  • Flexible hybrid: Offers both modes and lets you choose per meeting. The middle ground that appeals to most teams.

Pricing has also shifted. Free tiers are shrinking, but they're still meaningful differentiators—and the value gap between free and paid has widened. That matters if you're evaluating ROI for team deployments.

02

Granola: The Bot-Free Specialist

Granola

Silent, intelligent meeting notes without the bot

Granola has carved out a distinctive position: it's the strongest choice if you want zero visibility to other meeting participants. Its device-level capture model works by tapping directly into your system audio and microphone—no virtual attendee, no bot in the attendee list.

The workflow is intentionally different from transcription-first tools. Instead of dumping raw transcript into your lap, Granola prioritizes a hybrid notepad: you start by jotting your own notes, and the AI fills in context, transcription excerpts, and summaries around them. That approach resonates with people who want notes that reflect their own thinking, not just a playback of what was said.

Pricing as of September 2026:
  • Free: Basic free plan with unlimited meetings, but 30-day history window and limited archive access
  • Business: $14/user/month
  • Enterprise: $35/user/month

Strengths

  • Truly bot-free capture—no meeting visibility, ideal for sensitive conversations
  • User-written notes as the anchor; AI fills in context and details
  • Natural-language editing and cross-tool connectivity
  • Strong hybrid notepad model that differs from traditional transcription
  • Cross-platform availability (Windows, macOS, Android, iPhone)

Limitations

  • Desktop-first experience; mobile is secondary
  • Free plan history limited to 30 days
  • Less emphasis on team automation and integrations vs. Fireflies
  • Smaller ecosystem of third-party connections

03

Fathom: The Freemium Generalist

Fathom

Flexible capture modes with the most generous free plan

Fathom's biggest selling point is straightforward: it still offers one of the market's most generous free plans. As of September 2026, the free tier includes unlimited recordings, transcription, and instant AI summaries—features that many competitors now gate behind paid tiers.

Flexibility is the second strength. Fathom lets you choose your capture mode per meeting: use a bot when it's practical, or flip to bot-free mode when you need stealth. The bot-free option is currently in beta and Mac-only, which limits its reach, but it signals the company's willingness to compete on multiple fronts.

Where Fathom shows its limits is team features. Advanced summaries, detailed action items, and multi-user collaboration start in the paid tiers. For solo users or very small teams, this structure is unbeatable. For scaling organizations, you'll hit the paywall faster.

Pricing as of September 2026:
  • Free: Unlimited recordings, transcription, and basic summaries
  • Premium: $20/month or $16/year
  • Team: $19/month or $15/year
  • Business: $34/month or $25/year
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing

Strengths

  • Most generous free plan in the category—unlimited recordings and transcription
  • Flexible capture: choice of bot or bot-free per meeting
  • Fast setup and onboarding for new users
  • Straightforward, readable UI with minimal learning curve
  • Good value for individual users and small teams on free or Premium tier

Limitations

  • Bot-free mode is Mac-only and still in beta
  • Advanced summaries and action items require paid plans
  • Weaker team automation and workflow integrations vs. Fireflies
  • Limited native integrations outside common platforms
  • Free plan lacks advanced collaboration features for teams

04

Fireflies.ai: The Integration Powerhouse

Fireflies.ai

Deep integrations and workflow automation at scale

Fireflies.ai takes a different approach: it prioritizes deep integration into your existing stack. The company has invested heavily in connecting to CRM platforms, communication tools, project management systems, and custom workflows—40+ native integrations as of this writing.

The standout feature is automation. Once you've enabled Fireflies, it can automatically populate CRM fields, create action items in your project management tool, and distribute notes to the right channels—all without manual touching. For sales teams, ops-heavy organizations, and enterprise deployments, this automation pays dividends fast.

Capture is bot-based by default, but the company also offers bot-free options via desktop app, Chrome extension, and Google Meet SDK integrations. It's less opinionated than Granola on the topic, and that flexibility appeals to larger teams with mixed requirements.

Pricing as of September 2026:
  • Free: Limited transcription with core features
  • Pro: $18/month or $10/year
  • Business: $29/month or $19/year
  • Enterprise: $39/month (or higher for large deployments)

Unlimited transcription is available on all tiers, including free.

Strengths

  • 40+ native integrations: CRM, Slack, Hubspot, Salesforce, Microsoft, and more
  • Powerful automation: auto-populate CRM, create action items, route notes
  • Unlimited transcription on every tier, including free
  • AskFred (AI assistant) for natural-language question answering on call content
  • Strong team collaboration and permissions model
  • Best fit for sales, ops, and enterprise scaling

Limitations

  • Default bot-based capture; bot visibility is standard expectation
  • Bot-free modes are more fragmented (desktop app, extension, SDK)
  • Steeper learning curve due to integration complexity
  • Free tier is more limited than Fathom's; paid plans needed for full value
  • Requires buy-in from your CRM/tools to maximize ROI

Which Tool Should You Choose?

The Decision Framework

Choose Granola if... You need true bot-free capture and your team handles confidential, sensitive, or regulated conversations. Ideal for law firms, healthcare, finance, or any context where meeting participants shouldn't see a recording bot. Also excellent for solo users who prioritize privacy and a note-centric workflow.
Choose Fathom if... You want the easiest entry point and the best free tier. Perfect for individuals, freelancers, small teams testing the category, or anyone skeptical about ROI. The free plan is genuinely valuable—you can get real mileage without paying. Bonus: flexibility to try both bot and bot-free modes.
Choose Fireflies.ai if... You run a sales team, operate in a CRM-heavy environment (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive), or need meeting automation to populate fields, create tickets, or route notes automatically. The integration ecosystem and automation payoff compound as you scale. Best ROI for teams of 10+.

By Team Size and Budget

1-3 person team, tight budget: Fathom's free plan is unbeatable. Spend $0 until you actually need advanced features.

3-10 person team, mixed workflows: Fathom on Premium ($16-20/year) or Granola Business ($14/user/month) depending on whether you care about bot visibility.

10+ person team, sales or ops focus: Fireflies.ai Enterprise or Business tier. The integration ROI justifies the cost. Budget $200-400/month for a 10-person team.

Privacy-sensitive environment (legal, healthcare, finance): Granola Business ($14/user/month). Bot-free is non-negotiable here.

07

The Bottom Line

As of September 2026, AI meeting tools have splintered into clearly differentiated categories. You're no longer just choosing based on transcription quality—you're choosing based on capture philosophy and workflow integration depth.

Granola wins on privacy and the bot-free experience. It's the only true "invisible" option, and that matters more in certain industries than others.

Fathom wins on accessibility. The free plan is genuinely generous, and the flexibility to try both bot and bot-free modes makes it a safe first choice for teams uncertain about their needs.

Fireflies.ai wins on scale and automation. If your team lives in Salesforce or HubSpot, the integration value compounds fast. It's the most expensive entry point but offers the clearest ROI for sales and ops teams.

The right choice depends on three questions: (1) Do you need bot-free capture? (2) What's your budget? (3) How tightly integrated does this need to be with your existing tools? Answer those, and the decision becomes clear.

The best meeting tool isn't the fanciest—it's the one your team will actually use consistently. Start with Fathom's free plan, try Granola if you need privacy, or jump straight to Fireflies if you're already deep in a CRM. All three are solid bets in 2026.
