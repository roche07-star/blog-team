Meeting tools have matured significantly by 2026. The three leading contenders—Fireflies.ai, Otter.ai, and Fathom—now offer nearly identical core functionality: automatic recording, AI transcription, meeting summaries, and searchable transcripts. The real differentiation comes down to where each tool excels and what matters most to your workflow. This breakdown cuts through the noise and shows you exactly how these three stack up in October 2026.

01

Fireflies.ai: The Workflow Automation Leader

Fireflies.ai
Fireflies positions itself as the broadest meeting-to-work automation platform. As of October 2026, the platform handles Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, GoToMeeting, and others, with support for 100+ languages and native CRM integrations that directly funnel meeting insights into Salesforce, HubSpot, Asana, and Slack.

Pricing / October 2026

Free: Limited shared storage (approximately 400 minutes), limited AI summaries / Pro: $10/user/month (annual) / Business: $19/user/month / Enterprise: Custom pricing

What Fireflies Does Well

The standout feature announced in September 2026 is Fireflies Talk—unlimited voice dictation that lives inside Gmail, Slack, documents, and CRM systems. This pushes meeting intelligence beyond the meeting itself and into your everyday work.

Fireflies' integration depth is unmatched. Teams using Salesforce or HubSpot can sync call summaries, action items, and attendees directly into contact records. For knowledge-heavy organizations, Fireflies' searchable knowledge base across hundreds of meetings is a genuine productivity lever.

Language coverage (100+ languages, vendor claims of 99% accuracy for English and 95% for others) means global teams don't hit friction points with accents or non-English speakers.

Strengths

  • Broadest platform support (Webex, GoToMeeting, etc.)
  • Deepest CRM/workflow integrations
  • Unlimited voice dictation (Fireflies Talk)
  • 100+ language support with strong accuracy
  • Searchable knowledge base across many meetings

Limitations

  • Free plan storage reduced to ~400 minutes
  • Pricing step higher than Otter.ai
  • Transcription accuracy trails Otter (90-93% vs. 93-95%)
  • Complexity: many features take time to configure

02

Otter.ai: The Transcription Accuracy Choice

Otter.ai
Otter has positioned itself as the live transcription specialist since its launch. As of October 2026, the platform remains laser-focused on capturing speech accurately, delivering searchable transcripts across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. No major feature announcements landed in September 2026, but the product's positioning remains consistent: accurate transcription at a lower cost than competitors.

Pricing / October 2026

Free: 300 transcription minutes/month, 30-minute meeting limit, only 25 most recent conversations searchable / Pro: Approximately $8.33/user/month (annual) / Business / Enterprise: Custom pricing

What Otter Does Well

Independent benchmarks place Otter at 93-95% transcription accuracy—the highest of the three tools tested. For teams handling financial calls, technical discussions, or legal conversations, that margin matters. Otter's live transcription is real-time and visible to meeting participants, which creates an immediate record without manual note-taking.

Otter's pricing is the most attractive if you're comparing entry-level paid plans. At $8.33/month annually (Pro tier), it undercuts Fathom ($16/month) and Fireflies ($10/month) for straightforward transcription needs.

Language support remains limited to six languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese), which is a trade-off for Otter's accuracy focus.

Strengths

  • Highest independent transcription accuracy (93-95%)
  • Lowest-cost Pro tier ($8.33/month annual)
  • Real-time live transcription for meetings
  • Clean, simple interface
  • Searchable meeting history

Limitations

  • Free plan: only 25 most recent meetings searchable
  • Limited to 6 languages
  • Free plan: 300-minute monthly cap (restrictive)
  • Narrower platform support (no Webex)
  • Fewer workflow integrations than Fireflies

03

Fathom: The Unlimited Free Tier Winner

Fathom
Fathom offers the most generous free plan of the three tools. As of October 2026, users get unlimited recording, transcription, and storage at no cost. The catch: advanced AI summaries are capped at five calls per month on the free tier. Fathom focuses on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Slack Huddles with bot-free capture options on Mac.

Pricing / October 2026

Free: Unlimited recording / transcription / storage (5 advanced summaries/month) / Premium: $16/user/month (annual) / Team: $15/user/month (2-user minimum) / Business: $25/user/month

What Fathom Does Well

If you need a meeting tool with zero financial commitment and no artificial storage limits, Fathom wins. Solo practitioners, small teams testing the category, or organizations piloting before scaling will find enormous value in the free tier. The bot-free Mac capture mode (no bot joins the call) is a genuine technical advantage for sensitive conversations.

Fathom's paid tiers unlock unlimited advanced summaries, but even free users get standard summaries. The interface is clean and focused on meeting intelligence—clips, playlists, speaker timelines—without the complexity of CRM integrations.

Transcription accuracy (approximately 92%) sits between Otter (93-95%) and Fireflies (90-93%), making it a solid middle ground.

Strengths

  • Unlimited free recording, transcription, storage
  • No artificial meeting limits on free plan
  • Bot-free Mac capture (privacy advantage)
  • Clean, focused user interface
  • Solid transcription accuracy (92%)

Limitations

  • Advanced summaries limited to 5/month on free
  • Paid tier pricing higher than Otter
  • Narrower integration ecosystem
  • Language support (38 languages) less than Fireflies
  • Limited Android mobile support

05

Making the Right Choice

Choose Fireflies.ai if:

Your organization uses Salesforce or HubSpot and needs meeting summaries to flow directly into customer records. Global teams with non-English speakers benefit from the 100+ language support. You want voice dictation baked into your email and CRM. The higher pricing is justified by operational integration and time saved automating downstream work.

Choose Otter.ai if:

Transcription accuracy is non-negotiable—financial calls, technical meetings, legal conversations, or situations where accents and overlapping speakers are common. You're price-sensitive and need the lowest entry paid tier. Your team uses only Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams (no Webex requirement). Simplicity and straightforward transcription matter more than broad integrations.

Choose Fathom if:

You're an individual contributor, freelancer, or small team without budget approval for per-user subscriptions. You need unlimited free recording and transcription with no artificial storage limits. Privacy matters—bot-free Mac capture removes a bot from sensitive calls. You're piloting meeting intelligence before rolling out to larger teams. Five advanced summaries per month plus unlimited standard summaries is enough for your use case.

For Large Organizations:

Fireflies edges ahead with operational integration depth. Otter is the safer choice if transcription quality is your primary metric. Fathom is cost-effective for pilots or individual teams within the org.

The Bottom Line

As of October 2026, these three tools have converged on core capabilities—meeting recording, transcription, summaries, search, and action items. The competitive moat now sits in the details.

Fireflies dominates workflow automation and global language support. Otter owns transcription accuracy and cost. Fathom wins on free-tier generosity and simplicity. All three deliver reliable meeting capture for teams moving beyond manual note-taking.

Start with free trials to test against your actual meeting quality (background noise, accents, overlapping speech). Verify live pricing and plan limits before procurement—these products updated their plans multiple times during 2026. The right choice depends on whether your priority is automation, accuracy, or affordability.
