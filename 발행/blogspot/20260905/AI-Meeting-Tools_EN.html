Fireflies vs Otter.ai vs Fathom: Which AI Meeting Assistant Wins in 2026?

By Roche P. | September 2026 | 9 min read

According to recent industry data, professionals now spend over 18 hours per week in meetings—and most of that time produces scattered notes, missed action items, and zero searchable record. The AI meeting assistant market has exploded in response, with three platforms dominating the conversation: Fireflies, Otter.ai, and Fathom.

As of September 2026, the battle has intensified. Fireflies is pushing deep CRM integrations and workflow automation, Otter.ai has rolled out conversational knowledge engines and voice AI agents, and Fathom is winning users with bot-free recording options and the most generous free tier in the market.

Here's the breakdown: Fathom is the strongest free option, Fireflies delivers the best value for teams needing broad integrations, and Otter.ai remains the most mature choice for live transcription and conversational search. This comparison draws from the latest practical research available in September 2026, including third-party benchmarks, pricing trackers, and feature comparisons across all three platforms.

Fireflies.ai: The Automation and Integration Powerhouse

Fireflies positions itself as the workflow automation champion. As of September 2026, the platform supports over 40 integrations including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Notion, and major video conferencing tools. The standout feature is CRM write-back—Fireflies can automatically log call summaries, map action items to CRM fields, and update deal stages without manual input.

Pricing as of September 2026

Pro plan: $10/user/month (annual billing)

Free tier available with limited credits. Higher-tier plans available for advanced analytics and custom integrations.

Key Features in 2026

Fireflies has introduced "Talk to Fireflies," a voice-agent style search feature that lets users query meeting transcripts conversationally. The platform supports multilingual transcription, automated workflow triggers (e.g., "When a meeting mentions pricing, create a task in Asana"), and team analytics dashboards showing meeting time, speaker ratios, and sentiment trends.

According to third-party benchmarks documented in September 2026 research, Fireflies achieves approximately 92-94% transcription accuracy on clean audio, making it competitive but not the leader in raw accuracy.

Otter.ai: The Live Transcription and Search Leader

Otter.ai has been the household name in AI meeting notes since before the pandemic, and its 2026 updates show why it remains relevant. The platform's core strength is live, in-meeting transcription—you can watch the text appear in real-time, highlight key moments, and add comments during the call.

Pricing as of September 2026

Pro plan: $8.33/user/month (annual billing)

Monthly plans available around $16.99/month. Free tier includes limited monthly minutes.

Key Features in 2026

On April 28, 2026, Otter rolled out its "Conversational Knowledge Engine" and voice AI meeting agents. These features allow users to query across all past meetings with natural language ("Show me every time we discussed Q3 pricing") and deploy AI agents that can join calls, take notes, and answer questions autonomously.

The mobile experience is notably mature—Otter's iOS and Android apps support live transcription even when you're recording in-person meetings on your phone. However, transcription accuracy benchmarks from September 2026 place Otter at approximately 90-91%, behind both competitors.

Fathom: The Free-Tier Champion with Best-in-Class Summaries

Fathom has built its reputation on two pillars: an extremely generous free plan and the highest-quality AI summaries in the market. As of September 2026, Fathom's free tier includes unlimited recording and transcription with capped AI summary credits—a deal no competitor matches.

Pricing as of September 2026

Free plan: Unlimited recordings + transcripts, limited AI summaries

Premium plan: $15-19/user/month (pricing varies by source and billing cycle)

Key Features in 2026

In April 2026, Fathom introduced multiple recording modes: bot-free Mac desktop capture, bot-free audio-only, and traditional video bot modes. This flexibility is a game-changer for teams concerned about bot fatigue or clients uncomfortable with visible recording bots.

Fathom's AI summaries consistently rank highest in third-party evaluations. The platform generates structured notes with clear action items, decisions, and next steps. According to September 2026 benchmarks, Fathom achieves 95-96% transcription accuracy, the highest of the three tools. The platform also added account-wide AI search and integrations with ChatGPT, Claude, and the Model Context Protocol.

Transcription Accuracy Deep Dive

Based on third-party benchmarks documented in September 2026 research, transcription accuracy varies meaningfully across the three platforms. Fathom consistently achieves 95-96% accuracy on clean audio, Fireflies lands around 92-94%, and Otter.ai trails at 90-91%. These numbers matter for compliance-heavy industries, legal work, or any scenario where missing a few words creates real risk.

Integration Ecosystem

Fireflies wins the integration battle decisively. The platform supports deep CRM write-back to Salesforce and HubSpot, including custom field mapping and automated deal updates. It also connects to Slack, Notion, Asana, Trello, and dozens of other tools via native integrations and Zapier. Otter.ai offers basic integrations with major platforms but lacks the workflow automation depth. Fathom sits in the middle, with solid integrations to core tools plus new connections to ChatGPT and Claude for extended AI workflows.

Final Verdict: Which Tool Should You Choose?

Choose Fathom if: You're an individual user or small team prioritizing transcription accuracy, summary quality, and free-tier value. The unlimited free recordings make it a no-brainer for anyone testing AI meeting tools. The bot-free recording option is essential for client-facing calls or sensitive meetings where visible bots create friction.

Choose Fireflies if: You're a sales or customer success team that needs deep CRM integration, workflow automation, and team analytics
